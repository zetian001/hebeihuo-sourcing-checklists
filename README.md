# Hebei product sourcing: a practical RFQ checklist

This small, reusable checklist helps buyers compare offers for products sourced from Hebei, China. It is written for an actual purchasing decision: two prices can only be compared after the product specification, order size, packing, and delivery scope are aligned.

HebeiHuo maintains a [product-first Hebei sourcing catalog](https://hebeihuo.com/en/) with individual product pages, available public reference prices, and linked supplier profiles. Its listings are research starting points, not independent factory audits or binding offers.

## Before requesting a quote

1. Identify a **distinct product style**, then record dimensions, material, construction, color requirements, and any destination-market requirements. Do not treat a color or quantity variant as a new product.
2. Specify the **unit and configuration** for the quoted price. Ask whether it is per piece, set, carton, kilogram, or another unit; record the currency and validity date.
3. Request quantity tiers, **MOQ**, sample cost, sample lead time, and any separate tooling, logo, or customization charges.
4. Ask for **packing** details and a carton specification. For fragile glassware, include breakage protection; for bicycles, specify assembly level.
5. Keep **product price and freight separate**. Define the delivery term and destination before comparing landed cost.
6. Confirm the supplier's legal name, contact route, and the **actual production site** for the quoted item. A registered address alone does not establish manufacturing origin.
7. Agree on inspection criteria, lead time, payment milestones, and what happens if samples and bulk goods differ.

Copy the blank [RFQ template](RFQ-TEMPLATE.md) and send the same version to each candidate supplier. This keeps the comparison fair and makes missing answers visible.

## Limits

Public reference prices can be old, use different configurations, or exclude freight. Ask the supplier to confirm the current quote and availability. Supplier details sourced from public pages do not by themselves prove certification, capacity, or factory ownership.

This repository contains an original buyer checklist and a blank RFQ form. It does not reproduce supplier photos, scrape product feeds, or claim supplier verification.
